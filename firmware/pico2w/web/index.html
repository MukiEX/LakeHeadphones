Speaker arrangement

Stereo

USB stereo device (USB Audio 1.0) for consoles such as the Switch. Played on virtual front speakers in the room. Restarts the Pico; this page is not available in stereo mode (press BOOTSEL 3× to come back).

5.1

Six-channel USB sound card: front left/centre/right, two surrounds and LFE. Restarts the Pico when switching.

7.1

Eight-channel USB sound card: the surrounds become side speakers plus a separate rear pair. Set Windows' speaker setup to 7.1. Restarts the Pico when switching.

Room

Changes play immediately. Saving writes to flash, which causes a short gap in the sound.

Placement preset

Factory

The filters as they were made: surrounds at about 118°. In 7.1 the rear pair shares the surround position.

Theater

Surrounds at 136°, behind your shoulders. In 7.1 the rear pair goes far back, to 150°.

Custom

Your own placement from the sliders below.

Move the speakers

The sliders reshape the surround filters while you listen. Front, centre and room reverb stay as they are. Play something with busy surrounds (the walk-test WAV works well).

Surround speakers

Fine tune
Changes the time difference between your ears. Positive = smaller difference, further behind. Negative = larger, further forward.
Above 2 kHz at the far ear. Boost = smaller loudness difference (further behind), cut = larger.
How your outer ear colours sound from behind: more 1 kHz and 11 kHz, less 4.5 kHz. 100% = textbook amount; negative = the opposite.

Rear speakers 7.1 only

Fine tune

A rear pair in its own position needs extra processing (see Load on the System tab). At the surround position it costs nothing.

Bluetooth

Pair a different headset

Forgets the remembered headset and starts pairing. Put the new one in pairing mode close to the Pico; it picks the nearest headphones within a few seconds and you hear two rising tones.
