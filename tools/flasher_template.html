LakeHP flasher

Turns your own copy of the original headphone DLL into a ready-to-flash LakeHP firmware: the Pico 2 W build (Bluetooth headphones) or the Waveshare RP2350-USB-A build (USB DAC or 2.4 GHz headset dongle). Everything happens in this browser tab: the DLL is never uploaded anywhere.

1

Drop your DLL

The Lake Technology headphone engine DLL, version 1.20.0276 (the file name varies, e.g. randoapp.dll), or the project's open LakeHP_OpenRooms.dll. Only the 48 kHz room filters are used.

No DLL yet.
2

Pick a firmware

Built in: LakeHP __FW_VERSION__.

3

Build and flash

Waiting for the DLL.
  1. Hold BOOTSEL on the board while plugging it into USB (on the RP2350-USB-A: its USB-C port). A drive called RP2350 appears.
  2. Drag the downloaded .uf2 onto that drive. The board restarts by itself.
  3. Settings: open http://192.168.20.1 while it is plugged in (Windows or Linux).
  4. Plug the USB DAC or the headset's dongle into the USB-A port (a USB-C DAC needs a C-to-A adapter). LED: green = playing, yellow = starting, a red blip each second = nothing plugged in, magenta = that device has no playback format LakeHP can use.
  5. Put your headset in pairing mode, close to the Pico. After a few seconds it picks the nearest headphones, and you hear two rising tones. LED: fast flicker = pairing, a blip each second = reconnecting to the remembered headset, slow blink = connected, solid = playing.

The filters sit in their own flash area (0x10300000 on the Pico 2 W, 0x10100000 on the RP2350-USB-A), so later firmware updates can be flashed on their own without redoing this step.

Controls

  1. 1 press: next room (Small, Medium, Large)
  2. 2 presses: low-latency stereo on/off (skips the rooms)
  3. 3 presses: USB mode 5.1/7.1 ↔ stereo (for Switch and other consoles; restarts, remembered). Pick 5.1 or 7.1 on the settings page
  4. 4 presses: speaker placement Factory ↔ Theater (surrounds 136°, 7.1 rears 150°); 1 low beep = Factory, 2 = Theater
  5. 5 presses: settings page adapter on/off. With it on, open http://192.168.20.1 for stereo/5.1/7.1, rooms and the live Surround lab
  6. hold 2 s: forget the headset and pair a new one
  7. Headset buttons: play/pause, next and previous act as the PC's media keys
  8. hold 2 s: restart the stream to the USB DAC or dongle
